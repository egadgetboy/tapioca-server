A hotfix: easier reading, INFO listed in 4-hour blocks, stronger passwords, changes only from tapiOca's own page, and Plex's tuner kept beside a trusted proxy.

## New

- **Easier to read.** The page's text uses your device's own font, a little larger, with headings sized above their text. tapiOca's font stays on headings, tabs and buttons.
- **INFO in the guide** shows as 4-hour listings from midnight, each saying what it shows, in Plex's guide and every other. Before, Plex listed it every 2 minutes, with "Unknown" before the first.
- **Passwords** need at least 12 characters, as NIST's and OWASP's guidelines say, with no rules about symbols. The most common ones, and ones built from your name or tapiOca's, are turned away. Passwords set before keep working. Every password box has **Show**.
- **Can change their own PIN**, beside **Can change their own password**, for each person.
- **Searching logos** looks through every logo: "1" or "one" finds Number 1.

## Fixed

- **Changes from another site's page.** At home over plain http, browsers don't say which site sent a request, so another site's page could make changes while sign-in was off or someone was signed in. Every change now needs a header only tapiOca's own page sends. tapiOca's apps and scripts using its API aren't affected.
- **Plex beside a trusted proxy** lost the tuner and guide when Plex and the proxy shared an address. Plex there keeps them now. Anything else from the proxy's computer is still treated as from the internet.
- **INFO off the air.** Its film could be listed as a broken file, and then INFO showed "We'll be right back." It's never checked now, and a listing from before comes off.
- **Away from home with sign-in off** showed as a problem. Now it says your address works, and turning on sign-in is the step left.
- **What apps say** to someone watching is plain words, never an Admin's steps. A PIN tried on a new device says it works only on a device already signed in.
- **Trusted proxies,** said plainly. A proxy you just trusted no longer shows as "isn't a trusted proxy," and its alert ends.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.36.2.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. tapiOca adds one setting for each person (Can change their own PIN), so it copies the database first, to `data/backups/before-1.36.2-<date>.db`. Reload any page left open from before. A script of your own that changes things through the page's addresses (not the API's `/api/v1`) needs the header `X-Tapioca-Page: 1`.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.36.1` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). 1.36.1 runs on 1.36.2's database as it is, so nothing needs putting back. If Plex shares an address with a proxy you trusted, remove that proxy first, or 1.36.1 takes the tuner from Plex.
