Each Admin chooses Full or Light sign-in. Sign-in screens show only people who signed in there. No Admin tabs show before tapiOca knows who you are.

## New

- **Full or Light sign-in,** each Admin's own choice, in **Your account** (your name at the top of the page). **Full** is the default: your password every time. **Light**: your password once on each browser or device, then your PIN there, at home, gives your Admin tools. Changing it takes your password. So do passwords, PINs, making an Admin, backups and what reaches tapiOca from outside, even in Light.
- **Remove from this browser,** on the sign-in screen, takes an Admin off that browser's list.

## Fixed

- **The page's sign-in screen listed people who'd never signed in there,** Users too. Now it lists only Admins who signed in on that browser with their password. **Sign in with a password** is there for anyone.
- **An Admin's PIN on a trusted network hid the Admin tabs** with no word of why. In Light, your PIN now gives your Admin tools. Otherwise a note says the sign-in is for watching, with **Sign in with your password**.
- **The Admin tabs showed for a moment** before tapiOca said who was signed in. Now nothing only an Admin may use shows until it does.
- **The apps' Who's tuning in? listed people who'd never signed in on that device.** Now each person signs in once on each device, with a password, an invite code or **Link an app**. **Show on** is gone from the Access tab.
- **A new role now ends that person's sign-ins,** so they sign in again as what they are now.
- **From 1.37.1's checks:** **Check files** during a new station's first-day check now checks everything. A check that failed to start, or a deleted station's check, no longer runs on. Setup shows the tuner address Plex reaches. A failing station's alert says when its failures began.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.38.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database changes, so tapiOca saves a copy first (`backups/before-1.38.0-<date>.db`). Every Admin starts on **Full**. Admins' sign-ins from before the update are for watching: sign in with your password once to get your Admin tools back. To use your PIN for Admin tasks, choose **Light** in **Your account**. In the apps, people who were listed by **Show on** sign in once on each device. For someone with neither a password nor a PIN, make an invite code under **Devices**.

## Rolling back

Follow [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back): stop tapiOca, put `backups/before-1.38.0-<date>.db` back as `stationplay.db`, delete `stationplay.db-wal` and `stationplay.db-shm`, and use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.37.1`. Changes made since the update are lost. Sign-ins from before the update come back; sign-ins made since don't.
