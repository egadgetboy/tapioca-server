Search the Logs, sign back in with your PIN at home, sign-in cards that fit long names, and what tapiOca's apps need for Shuffle and jumping through Media.

## New

- **Search the Logs.** Type in **Search the log** to see only the lines that have those words, in any case. It covers the newest 500 lines, and with **Access** checked, the newest 2,000 sign-ins.
- **Your PIN after a time-out.** On a trusted network, the page lists you on a browser where you've signed in with your password. After an hour without activity signs you out, your PIN signs you back in there. Signing out on purpose still asks for your password next time. You need a PIN on your account (Access tab).
- **For tapiOca's apps:** a show's episodes in a random order (**Shuffle all**, or **Shuffle unwatched**), and where each letter, month, year or decade starts in a Media folder, for jumping through it. The apps that use these come next. Plex, Jellyfin and other apps on the tuner see only your stations.

## Fixed

- **The page's sign-in asked "Who's tuning in?"** That's the apps' question. The page asks **Who's signing in?** now. Its cards share the row: wide with few people, narrower with more. Long names show whole.
- **Buttons and labels were fully round at their ends.** The page's pills now have straight sides and slightly rounded corners, like the TV set in tapiOca's apps.
- **Media sorted by release date** put "Unknown" and other dates that aren't a year first. They count as undated now, last.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.37.0.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. Nothing in the database changes.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.36.4` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). 1.36.4 runs on 1.37.0's database as it is. The Logs search and the PIN after a time-out on a trusted network go away.
