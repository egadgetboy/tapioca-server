In tapiOca's apps, a program someone may not watch is now blocked for them while it's on, on every station, and their station comes back on its own afterward.

## New

- **Programs are checked as they air.** Say you allow Saturday Toons for your daughter, whose level is Kid. At 9:00 a TV-14 episode comes on: her app says "This program is restricted. Your station will resume automatically when permitted programming returns." At 10:00 the next cartoon starts, and she's watching again, with nothing to do. Everyone else watches the whole time, as one shared broadcast. The server checks what's really playing and refuses the stream's pieces of that program, plus a few seconds either side.
- **"Restricted program" in her guide,** with its times and nothing else, wherever a program is listed. Search never finds a station by one.
- **Allowing a station never allows what's on it.** Which stations someone sees is as before. To let a child watch an unrated home video, allow that title for them under **What they see**. It then counts in Media and on stations alike.
- **On a station you allowed by hand,** its Up Next Banner and cards say "Stay tuned" rather than name a program someone it's allowed for may not watch. Its commercials and trailers go no higher than their level.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.39.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database doesn't change, so tapiOca makes no copy. **Check the stations you allowed by hand.** Programs on them above a person's level, unrated ones included, are now blocked for them while they're on. To show a title anyway, allow it for them under **What they see**. Apps that don't yet know "Restricted program" show an error during one; tune in again after it.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.38.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). The database didn't change, so there's no copy to put back and nothing is lost. Stations allowed by hand then play everything on them again for everyone they're allowed for.
