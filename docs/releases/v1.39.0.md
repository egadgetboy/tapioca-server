In tapiOca's apps, a program someone's limits don't allow is now blocked for them while it's on, on every station, and the station comes back on its own afterward.

## New

- **Programs are checked as they air.** Say you allow Saturday Toons for your daughter, whose level is Kid. At 9:00 a TV-14 episode comes on. Her app says "This program is restricted. Your station will resume automatically when permitted programming returns." At 10:00 the next cartoon starts, and she's watching again without doing anything. Everyone else watches the whole time, as one shared broadcast. tapiOca goes by what's really playing. Her app is also blocked a few seconds before the program and about 15 seconds after it. If a still card follows, that can be up to 35 seconds.
- **"Restricted program" in her guide,** with its times and nothing else, wherever a program is listed. Searching for its title doesn't show that station as on now.
- **Allowing a station never allows what's on it.** Which stations someone sees is as before. To let a child watch an unrated home video, allow that title for them under **What they see**. It then counts in Media and on stations alike.
- **On a station you allowed by hand,** nothing on screen names a program someone it's allowed for may not watch. There's no Up Next Banner before it, and cards say "Stay tuned" or leave the title out. Its commercials and trailers go no higher than their level.
- **What doesn't get this:** Plex, Jellyfin and other apps on the tuner, and a station's stream address played at home (in VLC, say), show every program, as before.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.39.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database doesn't change, so tapiOca makes no copy. **Check the stations you allowed by hand.** Programs on them above a person's level, unrated ones included, are now blocked for them while they're on. To show a title anyway, allow it for them under **What they see**. Apps that don't yet know "Restricted program" show an error during one. Tune in again after it.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.38.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). The database didn't change, so there's no copy to put back and nothing is lost. Stations allowed by hand then play everything on them again for everyone they're allowed for.
