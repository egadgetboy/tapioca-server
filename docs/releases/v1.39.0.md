A station you allow for someone by hand is now blocked for them while something above their limits is scheduled on it, and comes back when it's clear.

## New

- **Stations allowed by hand follow limits.** Say you allow Saturday Toons for your daughter, whose level is Kid, and a TV-14 episode is scheduled. She can't tune in from the time it shows up in the next 48 hours (the guide's window) until it has aired. Then Saturday Toons is back. Under **What they see**, the Access tab says when it's back, or that it won't come back on its own.
- **Someone already watching is stopped** in tapiOca's apps when a schedule change or a new level means they can't watch that station anymore. Everyone else watches on, as one shared stream.
- **On a station allowed by hand,** a program that can't play is replaced only by one from the next 48 hours, and commercials and trailers go no higher than what's on in them.
- **Guides for someone with limits** start at what's on now and never show what aired before. For a station allowed by hand, they show only the next 48 hours, and the page leaves out what it's made of.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.39.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database doesn't change, so tapiOca makes no copy. **Check the stations you allowed by hand.** One that always has something above a person's level, such as home videos that aren't rated on a Kid level, is now blocked for them. To show it, allow those shows and movies for them in **What they see**, under **Allowed for (their name) in Media**, or choose a level that includes them. Stations that follow a person's level are as before.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.38.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). The database didn't change, so there's no copy to put back and nothing is lost. Stations allowed by hand show for everyone they're allowed for again, whatever they play.
