This release makes first-time setup work from the start, adds INFO on station 99, and shares Media with tapiOca's apps from the start on a new tapiOca.

## New

- **INFO, station 99.** A new tapiOca starts with it, so Plex's DVR setup has a station to find. It shows the time, the date and tapioca.tv over pearls flying through tapiOca's ring, to a light tune. A panel changes every 15 seconds: what's new in this version, tapiOca's apps, the numbers (counts of your stations and shared Media, never titles) and a tip. Everyone who can watch your stations sees it, so it never shows your server's addresses or how it's doing. It plays without Plex. Updating? Add it under **The INFO station** on the **Add to Plex** tab.
- **Setup opens at once.** Step 1 checks the server itself, your media folder included, and never waits on Plex. Plex has its own step, which says what's wrong when tapiOca can't reach it.
- **Media in the apps, on to start:** a new tapiOca shares every Media folder, new ones too, until an Admin chooses otherwise. Updating from a tapiOca that never chose? Nothing is shared until you say so: setup asks once, with every folder ticked, and going on shares them. A choice already saved is kept.
- **The Viewing Level new people start on,** on the **Access** tab and in setup, and a level for each person as you add them.
- **Test the connection** from the **Access** tab, and plainer limits for watching at once.
- **A header pill while a newer tapiOca is out,** even after **Not now**. The update check looks every 4 hours.

## Fixed

- Plex's problems read as plain sentences, never "Plex request for … failed ()". They say when something other than Plex answers at PLEX_URL, and a Plex that isn't answering no longer holds up a page.
- **Copy** works in setup's window when the page is opened by its address on your home network.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.34.1.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. The database's structure doesn't change, so no backup is made, and none is needed: tapiOca only saves a few new settings, which 1.34.0 ignores. On `:1.34`? You get this update too. Like any update, it never adds INFO or shares Media by itself.

## Rolling back

To go back to 1.34.0, use its image, `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.34.0`, in place of `:latest` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). Your stations and settings are kept. INFO stays as a station that can't play in 1.34.0: delete it there, or update again. A tapiOca that never chose its Media folders shares none in 1.34.0.
