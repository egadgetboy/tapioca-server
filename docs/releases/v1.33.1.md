This release explains where your PIN works on StationPlay's page, and adds StationPlay's privacy notice and what Plex's terms require.

## Fixed

- **Where your PIN works.** Through StationPlay's internet address (your reverse proxy or tunnel), the page always asks for your password, even at home. The sign-in page now says so, and that your PIN works at its home-network address, once you've signed in there with your password.

## New

- **A privacy notice,** linked at the foot of every page (and as `PRIVACY.md`), says what StationPlay keeps on your hardware, for how long, and what it contacts. It sends nothing to its developer.
- **Plex's terms.** The README and first-run setup's Plex step say what Plex's terms require, with a link to them: Plex Media Server for personal use, on hardware and storage you own.

## Good to know

- **After updating from a version before 1.32.0,** each browser needs your password once before your PIN works there.
- **Coming next:** 1.34.0 moves every install to StationPlay's published image, so later updates take one step.

## Updating

`stationplay-1.33.1.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`), `stationplay.yaml` is for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

Unzip `stationplay-1.33.1.zip` over `src`, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.33.1` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations, settings, sign-ins and Plex token are kept. From 1.33.0, nothing in the database changes, so no copy of it is made. From an earlier version, StationPlay first saves `data/backups/before-1.33.1-<date>.db`: to go back, follow 1.33.0's Rolling back with that file. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).

## Rolling back

To go back to 1.33.0 (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#rolling-back)):

1. Stop StationPlay.
2. Unzip `stationplay-1.33.0.zip` over `src`.
3. Change `image:` back to `stationplay:1.33.0`, and start it.

Everything is kept as it is.
