This release fixes Docker's own address as a trusted proxy, offers **Trust this proxy** where tapiOca finds your reverse proxy isn't trusted yet, and says plainly when MEDIA_DIR holds a whole volume line.

## Fixed

- **Docker's own address as a trusted proxy.** Listed for a reverse proxy in another container on the same server, it made tapiOca treat Plex, other containers and tapiOca's apps at home as from the internet, since they reach tapiOca through that address too. Plex lost your stations, and with signing in off the page could lock you out. Now what comes from it with no forwarding headers is taken as from that address, at home, as before it was listed.
- **Trust this proxy.** When tapiOca checks its outside address and finds it came through a reverse proxy that isn't trusted yet, it names the proxy and offers **Trust this proxy** right there: in the setup's Watching away from home step, and on the Access tab. Before, it sent you to the Access tab mid-setup. It trusts only the address its own check came through, and says first if that's Docker's own address. It isn't offered for an internet address, when 10 are listed, or for Plex's address or one that used the tuner lately.
- **Locked out?** The reset-access file now clears Trusted proxies too.
- **MEDIA_DIR holding a whole volume line** (such as `/mnt/pool/media:/media:ro`): Checking your server says so, and what to set instead.
- **The YAML and Compose files** say to leave MEDIA_DIR as `/media`, and to change only the folder on your server in each volumes line.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.36.1.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. Nothing in the database changes, so no copy is made.

## Rolling back

To go back to 1.36.0, use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.36.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). Nothing needs putting back. A proxy you trusted stays listed. If it's Docker's own address, remove it first: 1.36.0 cuts Plex off through it.
