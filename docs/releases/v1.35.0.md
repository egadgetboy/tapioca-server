This release adds Media folders of your own: tapiOca reads your shows and movies straight from your folders, with or without Plex.

## New

- **Media folders of your own.** On the **Setup** tab, under **Your own folders**, choose **Add a Media folder**: a name, Shows or Movies, and its folders inside the media folder. It works as one of Plex's does, for stations, filters, smart stations, collections, and Media in tapiOca's apps. Lay it out as Sonarr, Radarr, Plex, Jellyfin and Emby do (see [Media folders of your own](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#media-folders-of-your-own)).
- **Details from the files themselves:** NFO files beside the videos first, then tags inside the files, then folder and file names. Pictures come from beside the videos, then from covers inside the files. Nothing is looked up online, and nothing is guessed.
- **Viewing Levels work as with Plex.** Ratings are read however tools write them ("Rated R", "US:R", "UK:18"), and a level that hides a genre, tag, collection or studio hides the titles that have it.
- **Plex is optional.** With a Media folder of your own, tapiOca runs without `PLEX_URL`, and setup's Plex step says so. Watching in Plex's Live TV still needs Plex and Plex Pass. Jellyfin, other apps and tapiOca's own apps need neither.
- **The Setup tab:** Add to Plex is now **Setup**, and the addresses for Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi and other apps are under **Tuner integration**.
- **Kept up to date by itself:** read again every 6 hours, when you choose **Read again**, and when Sonarr or Radarr brings a replacement tapiOca asked for, never while anyone's watching. A file renamed, moved or replaced with a better copy keeps its place and what people watched of it. A share or folder that can't be reached never empties your stations.

Not yet in these Media folders: skipping intros and credits (on stations and in tapiOca's apps), and pictures for titles with none of their own. Matching titles, and fixing what's wrong, come later. Without Plex, an update to a station waits until the guide other apps downloaded runs out (up to 2 days), so their guides stay right.

## Fixed

- **Setup's media folder check** says where your media is inside tapiOca, and what to set `MEDIA_DIR` to, when `MEDIA_DIR` names the folder's place on your server instead.
- **Away from home, before signing in is set up,** tapiOca's apps say "This tapiOca client can't reach the server. Please check back later." instead of an Admin's steps.
- **Without Plex:** Media in tapiOca's apps, Broken files and the Stations tab no longer say Plex failed or lost what was never Plex's, and the YAML's sample `PLEX_URL` and `PLEX_TOKEN`, left in place, no longer count as Plex set up.
- **Who can use tapiOca** says plainly what at home means (port 3310: your network, or a VPN into it) and that nobody can use tapiOca from the internet until signing in is on.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.35.0.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. This update adds four tables to the database, so tapiOca first copies it to `backups/before-1.35.0-<date>.db` in its data folder. Your stations, settings and people stay as they are, and Media folders of your own appear only when you add one. The **Add to Plex** tab is now **Setup**. On `:1.34`? Change it to `:1.35` (or `:latest`) to get this update. The TrueNAS file is now `tapioca-server.yaml` (it was `stationplay.yaml`). A TrueNAS install made from the old file needs no change.

## Rolling back

To go back to 1.34.1, follow [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back): stop tapiOca, put `backups/before-1.35.0-<date>.db` in place of `stationplay.db` (deleting `stationplay.db-wal` and `stationplay.db-shm`), and use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.34.1`. Stations, settings and people return to how they were before the update; Media folders of your own, and stations made from them, are gone. Your folders and files are never changed, so adding them again in 1.35.0 later reads them as before.
