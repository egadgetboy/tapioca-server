Browsing Media in tapiOca's apps is quick again, and folders no longer say "tapiOca can't show your Media right now" while pictures load.

## Fixed

- **Folders open on the first try.** While many pictures were being made, a Movies or Shows folder could take longer than tapiOca waits, and the apps said they couldn't load it until you tried again.
- **Scrolling is fast again.** Pictures for the apps are made the way 1.41.0 made them: Plex resizes its own, and tapiOca keeps them in memory. Changed art still shows after its folder is browsed again, or after a scan for a poster file you replaced.
- **A picture you scrolled past isn't made.** tapiOca no longer asks Plex for a picture the app gave up on while it waited its turn.
- **Less asked of Plex.** For someone with no limits, a poster no longer makes tapiOca look up the title's details when the list it came from already said what's needed.
- **A Plex hiccup isn't a missing picture.** When Plex fails a picture, tapiOca says to try again, so the updated apps (Android 0.12.1, Apple 0.11.1, Roku 0.15.1) keep their placeholder and ask on the next screen, instead of fetching a bigger picture. If Plex refuses tapiOca's sign-in for pictures, Admins get one alert.

## Removed

- **Pictures kept on disk, WebP, drawing ahead and Station logos sized for the apps,** with **Pictures for tapiOca's apps** on the Access tab (`PICTURE_CACHE_GB` is ignored). About 30 seconds after it starts, tapiOca deletes `data/cache/pictures` once, if it's 1.42's, freeing the space you gave it (2 GB to start). If `data/cache` is a link to another disk, it's cleaned there and the link stays; if `data/cache/pictures` itself is a link, only the link goes, and the log says what it pointed to is yours to delete. Apps that ask for WebP get JPEG.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.43.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). From 1.42.1, the database doesn't change, so tapiOca makes no copy. From 1.42.0 or older, 1.42.1's sign-in change comes with this update, so tapiOca saves a copy first: `data/backups/before-1.43.0-<date>.db`. 1.42.1's notes say what that change means.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.42.1` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)), whichever version you came from. It runs on this database as it is, so there's nothing to put back and nothing is lost, and it starts with no pictures saved on disk, making them again as they're asked for.

Going further back than 1.42.1 needs the copy: stop tapiOca, put `data/backups/before-1.43.0-<date>.db` back as `data/stationplay.db`, delete `data/stationplay.db-wal` and `data/stationplay.db-shm`, then start the image you had. Sign-ins and changes made since the update are lost.

For developers: every app sign-in now says the person's ID (`user.id`). The tests retired with 1.42's pictures, and every changed expected result, are listed in `docs/notes/retired-tests-1.43.0.md`.
