In tapiOca's apps, one check now decides what each person may watch, on Stations, in Media and in every list, and it keeps checking a movie or episode while it plays.

## New

- **Limits apply while a title plays.** Say you move your son to the Kid level halfway through an R-rated movie. Within a few seconds tapiOca stops sending it, and his app stops once what it has buffered runs out. Before, a title was checked only as it started, so it played to the end. A title his new level still allows plays on. The access log notes each stop.
- **Removing someone stops what they're watching,** at once, as does turning off a Media folder they're watching from.
- **One check in tapiOca's apps.** Stations as they air, Media as it starts and while it plays, and every list ask the same check. Hidden episodes and Clean audio will use it once they're built: a hidden episode will be refused in Media and blocked while it airs, with its Station staying, and a level that needs Clean audio will block a program until its Clean audio is ready. Plex, Jellyfin and other apps on the tuner aren't covered, as before.

## Fixed

- **The station editor's previews** (the Intro Bumper and the Up Next Banner) no longer play or name what's on a Station an Admin blocked for the person making the preview.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.40.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database doesn't change, so tapiOca makes no copy. Apps that don't yet know "This title is restricted." show their usual "can't play" message when a title stops this way.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.39.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). The database didn't change, so there's no copy to put back and nothing is lost. Titles are then checked only as they start, as before.
