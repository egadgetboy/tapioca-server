This release lets a Viewing Level, or one person, hide titles by genre, tag, collection or studio in StationPlay's apps, and plays only rated commercials and trailers on stations that someone with limits can see.

## New

- **Hide by what a title is.** A Viewing Level can hide any show or movie with a genre, tag (Plex's labels), collection or studio you name, whatever its rating. Choose **Edit** beside a level on the **Access** tab.
- **What they see.** The button beside each person's level, formerly **Stations**, also hides genres, tags, collections, studios and shows or movies for just them, and can allow a show or movie in Media that their level would hide.
- **Commercials and trailers under limits.** Rate each clip folder under **Viewing Levels**. On a station someone with limits can see, a clip plays only if its folder is rated at or below the station's highest-rated program. Other stations play every clip, as before.
- **Parental limits,** a new part of the README, says what StationPlay enforces in its apps and on its page, and what it can't (Plex, Jellyfin and anything else on the tuner; devices without PINs).
- **"STATION 9"** on the Intro Bumper and Station ID card.

## Good to know

- **No clip folder is rated yet.** Until you rate them, stations that someone with limits can see skip their commercials and trailers (a plain card fills a clip already chosen), and an alert says how many stations do.
- **Someone with anything hidden for them** only watches, like someone on a level with limits: they can't make stations.
- **The first look at a big Media folder,** for someone limited by genre or tag, shows some titles at first, and the rest as Plex answers StationPlay about them.

## Updating

`stationplay-1.33.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`), `stationplay.yaml` is for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

Unzip `stationplay-1.33.0.zip` over `src`, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.33.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations, settings, sign-ins and Plex token are kept, and everyone sees the same stations and titles as before. As it starts, 1.33.0 saves a copy of the database in `data/backups` as `before-1.33.0-<date>.db`, then updates it. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).

## Rolling back

To go back to 1.32.1 (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#rolling-back)):

1. Stop StationPlay.
2. In the data folder, replace `stationplay.db` with `backups/before-1.33.0-<date>.db`, and delete `stationplay.db-wal` and `stationplay.db-shm` if they're there.
3. Unzip `stationplay-1.32.1.zip` over `src`.
4. Change `image:` back to `stationplay:1.32.1`, and start it.

Everyone stays signed in, and everything is as it was just before the update. Anything changed since is lost. (1.32.1 also starts on 1.33.0's database as it is, but it ignores what 1.33.0 hides by genre, tag, collection, studio or title, and every clip plays again.)
