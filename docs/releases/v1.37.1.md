A fix: a very large smart station no longer freezes tapiOca, old alerts clear with an update, INFO plays only in tapiOca's apps, and Setup shows your tuner's real address.

## Fixed

- **A smart station of tens of thousands of episodes could freeze tapiOca** for minutes at a time, page and streams included, while its files were being checked. Now a new station's first day of programs is checked right away, and the rest as the file checks reach them, soonest to air first. **Check files** still checks everything, a few files at a time.
- **An alert from before an update stayed on.** INFO's "keeps failing to start" from 1.36.3, which couldn't find INFO's film, was still listed on 1.37.0, which plays it. A station's alert now clears with an update, and comes back if the station fails again. It also says when the failures began, instead of "in the last 10 minutes".
- **INFO's alert pointed to Plex and your files.** INFO's film is part of tapiOca, so its alert says neither is the cause.
- **Plex, Emby and other apps could still tune INFO** if they listed it before 1.36.4. INFO is for tapiOca's apps, so the tuner now refuses it once you have a station of your own.
- **INFO can't turn GPU encoding off.** If INFO ever fails on your GPU, it plays on the CPU for the rest of that viewing, and it never counts toward turning the GPU off for every station.
- **Setup showed `http://<your NAS>:3310`** for the tuner, the guide and the playlist when you opened tapiOca's page from the internet. It now shows the address Plex's Live TV & DVR uses for tapiOca's tuner.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.37.1.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. Nothing in the database changes. If you skipped 1.36.4, choose **Scan for channels** once in Plex's Live TV & DVR settings, so Plex lets INFO go.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.37.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). 1.37.0 runs on 1.37.1's database as it is. Big smart stations can freeze tapiOca again, the tuner plays INFO to any app that asks, and Setup opened from the internet shows `<your NAS>` again.
