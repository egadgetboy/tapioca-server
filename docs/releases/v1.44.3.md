Sign out in tapiOca's apps always takes you off that device's Who's tuning in? list, even when your sign-in there had already ended.

## Fixed

- **Sign out always takes you off the list.** If your sign-in on a shared device had ended before you pressed Sign out (you changed your password on another device, your role or PIN changed, someone else signed in there, or it went a day unused), Sign out left you on that device's Who's tuning in? list, where anyone could pick you if you had no PIN. Now it always takes you off, and ends every sign-in of yours on that device. A sign-in that has ended does nothing else. Nothing changes on screen, and the apps don't need an update. If the app had already gone back to Who's tuning in? by itself before you pressed Sign out, you're still listed: pick yourself, then Sign out.
- **A new release signing key.** From 1.44.3, releases are signed by the key in `release-signing.pub`. The key that signed 1.44.2 is retired and no longer trusted: check 1.44.2 by its `SHA256SUMS`, as [Checking a release's signature](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#checking-a-releases-signature) says.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.44.3.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database gains one table, so tapiOca backs it up first, as `backups/before-1.44.3-<date>.db`.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.44.2` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). It runs on this database as it is and ignores the new table, so nothing is lost; or put the backup copy back first, as Rolling back says. Updating to 1.44.3 again rebuilds the table from the sign-ins it finds.
