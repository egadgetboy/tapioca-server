Disc subtitles that tapiOca draws into a copy for its apps now fit the picture, at the right size.

## Fixed

- **Disc subtitles in a copy.** When tapiOca makes a copy of a movie or show for its apps with a Blu-ray's or a DVD's subtitles turned on, it draws them into the picture. It drew them at the size of the disc's whole frame. In a widescreen movie whose black bars were cut away, they looked too big, and their last line could fall off the bottom. In a smaller copy (away from home, on a slow connection, or of a 4K movie), they often didn't show at all. They now fit inside the picture, as they already did on Stations.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.44.1.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database doesn't change, so tapiOca doesn't back it up first.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.44.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). It runs on this database as it is, so there's nothing to put back and nothing is lost.
