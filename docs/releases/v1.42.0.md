Posters and backdrops in tapiOca's apps now come quickly, even right after a restart or an update.

## New

- **Pictures are kept on the server's disk.** Each poster and backdrop made for the apps is kept in `data/cache/pictures`, so tapiOca doesn't make every picture again after a restart or an update. It's made once however many devices ask for it at once.
- **Space for pictures**, under **Access**, in **Pictures for tapiOca's apps**: 2 GB to start. When it's full, the pictures viewed longest ago make room. Enter 0 to keep none. Pictures are never in backups, since tapiOca can make them again.
- **A changed poster shows.** A new poster in Plex shows after the next time its Media folder is browsed, and a poster file you replace shows after the next scan.
- **Smaller pictures for the apps.** Apps may ask for WebP, which is smaller at the same quality, and each kind of picture comes in a few widths of its own. Station logos come at the size the apps draw them.
- **Drawn ahead.** While no one's watching, tapiOca makes the newest titles' pictures and each person's latest, so the first screens are quick.

Today's apps work as before and get the speed now. WebP and the new widths come with the apps' new look.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.42.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database doesn't change, so tapiOca makes no copy.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.41.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). The database didn't change, so there's no copy to put back and nothing is lost. 1.41.0 ignores the `data/cache/pictures` folder; delete it to free the space.
