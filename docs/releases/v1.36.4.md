A hotfix: INFO plays again, night mode cuts deep rumble, the log says what really happened, and the page's rough edges are smoothed.

## Fixed

- **INFO said "We'll be right back."** Since 1.35.0, tapiOca has mistaken INFO's film for one in the Media folders it reads itself and looked for it there. It was never there, so station 99 showed its "We'll be right back" card. tapiOca now finds the film where it keeps it.
- **INFO in Plex and other apps.** INFO is for tapiOca's apps. Plex's tuner, its guide and the playlist other apps use leave it out once you have a station of your own. Until then it's listed, so a new tapiOca can still be added to Plex.
- **Night mode let deep rumble through.** It now cuts sound under 50 Hz, steeply, so explosions don't shake the room or pull the voices down. Bass from 100 Hz up is as it was. This is the sound tapiOca makes for Apple's and Roku's apps, and for Media copies made with night mode. tapiOca for Android 0.6.3 makes it the same way on the device.
- **The log got three things wrong.** It said "stopped" for a program that played to its end. It says "finished" now. When a converted copy went back to the file itself, it said "switched to a smaller version… to fit its connection". That's now an ordinary "started" line, and the connection is named only when it was the reason. And a copy with subtitles drawn in now says which ones, and why.
- **Passwords:** at least 8 characters (12 in 1.36.2 and 1.36.3), still with no rules about symbols, and still never one of the most common.
- **The page:** one list of Media folders on the Setup tab, each with a switch for tapiOca's apps. Even sound's switch is in setup's "Media in tapiOca's apps" step too. Episodes on your stations always have even sound; the switch is for Media. Top people shows full names. Checkboxes are teal. The down arrow shows only when there's more to see (Safari showed it under an empty page). The browser's tab shows tapiOca's mark and says "tapiOca Server".

## Updating

`tapioca-1.36.4.zip` is the source. Update as the banner, or [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating), says. Nothing in the database changes. Then, in Plex's Live TV & DVR settings, choose **Scan for channels** once, so Plex lets INFO go.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.36.3` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). 1.36.3 runs on 1.36.4's database as it is. INFO then shows its "We'll be right back" card again, and night mode lets rumble through.
