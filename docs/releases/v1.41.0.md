Media in tapiOca's apps now knows each title's content rating everywhere it knows the year, ready for the new look to show it beside the year.

## New

- **Every Media list, search, the Resume row, Up next and each title's page** now carry a title's rating, such as PG-13 or TV-14, beside its year. An episode's is its show's, or its own when that's stricter, the same way limits judge it. A title someone may not see is never listed, so neither is its rating.
- **Ratings read the way people know them:** Plex's country mark is left off ("us/TV-MA" is "TV-MA"), and an unrated title has none.

Today's apps don't show it yet; the apps' new look will. Station guides get the rating with the apps' own routes, in a later release.

## Updating

`tapioca-1.41.0.zip` is the source. Update the way the banner says, or see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#updating). The database doesn't change, so tapiOca makes no copy. Apps that don't know the new field ignore it.

## Rolling back

Use the image `ghcr.io/egadgetboy/tapioca-server:1.40.0` (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/tapioca-server#rolling-back)). The database didn't change, so there's no copy to put back and nothing is lost.
