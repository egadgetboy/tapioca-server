This release lets you choose which devices can use the tuner, signs in to StationPlay's page with a PIN at home, sends alerts to Discord, and makes Night mode only ever turn the sound down.

## New

- **Tuner access.** On the **Access** tab, choose who can use the tuner that Plex, Jellyfin and other apps use: **Any device on my home network** (as before) or **Only these devices**, listed by address. **Plex server only** lists just Plex, after showing you its address. **Recent tuner connections** shows who used it in the last 7 days, with **Allow** beside each. StationPlay's own apps, signed in, never need listing.
- **Sign in with your PIN at home.** On a browser where you've signed in with your password, your PIN signs you in again after the page's hour without activity. From the internet, it's always your password. After 5 wrong PINs, wait 15 minutes. After 10 on one browser, use your password there.
- **Alerts in Discord.** Paste a Discord webhook address under **Notifications** on the **Logs** tab.
- **Open-source notices** for what StationPlay and its image include, such as FFmpeg, linked at the bottom of the page.
- **Link an app** fills in the code's dash as you type.

## Fixed

- **Night mode never makes anything louder.** It only turns the sound down: loud scenes the most, voices a little. This is the sound StationPlay makes for the Apple and Roku apps. Android 0.4.1 already does this on the phone.
- **Back up now** that fails says why in a sentence, and the log says what went wrong.

## Changed

- **Wording and logos.** The page calls on-demand titles **Media**, and Plex's libraries **Media folders**. Examples use made-up titles. 49 built-in logos were redrawn and 18 renamed, so none looks like a real network's. A station using one shows its new look. Two logo categories are now **Movie stations** and **Classic stations**.

## Good to know

- **Signing out on purpose,** a new password, or 10 wrong PINs means that browser needs your password before your PIN works there again. So do browsers you signed in on before 1.32.0, once.
- **Before choosing Only these devices,** turn sign-in on (or anyone at home can change it), and list Plex or choose **Plex server only** (or Plex can't show your stations). The tab warns you about both.
- **StationPlay's apps** get stream addresses of their own at home within 10 minutes of the update, or when reopened. Wait that long before choosing Only these devices.

## Updating

`stationplay-1.32.0.zip` is the app (it unzips to `src`), `stationplay.yaml` is for TrueNAS SCALE, and `docker-compose.yml` is for everything else.

Unzip `stationplay-1.32.0.zip` over `src`, then change only the version in `image:` to `stationplay:1.32.0` (see [Updating](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#updating)). Your stations, settings, sign-ins and Plex token are kept. As it starts, 1.32.0 saves a copy of the database in `data/backups` as `before-1.32.0-<date>.db`, then updates it. For a new install, see [Install](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#install).

## Rolling back

Earlier versions are no longer published here. To go back to 1.31.2, with its zip or `src` folder that you kept (see [Rolling back](https://github.com/egadgetboy/stationplay#rolling-back)):

1. Stop StationPlay.
2. In the data folder, replace `stationplay.db` with `backups/before-1.32.0-<date>.db`, and delete `stationplay.db-wal` and `stationplay.db-shm` if they're there.
3. Unzip your `stationplay-1.31.2.zip` over `src` (or put back the `src` folder you kept).
4. Change `image:` back to `stationplay:1.31.2`, and start it.

Everyone stays signed in, and everything is as it was just before the update. Anything changed since is lost. 1.31.2 has no Tuner access, so any device on your home network can use the tuner again, and the page signs in with passwords only.
