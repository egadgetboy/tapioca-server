### The privacy notice

The full notice is served by your server at `/privacy` (**Privacy**, at the bottom of the page), and is `PRIVACY.md` in the repository. In short:

- **tapiOca's developer runs no service for it** and receives nothing from it: no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
- **It keeps, in its data folder:** your Stations and settings (with Sonarr's and Radarr's keys and alert addresses); people, their Viewing Levels, sign-ins and devices, with passwords and PINs as salted hashes; what each person watches, and their stats; Plex users' names and what they watched on your Stations; the access log; trusted networks, proxies and names; reported problems; and backups. Admins can see all of it. Stats are cleared after 400 days, the apps' problems after 30, and handled reports after 90.
- **Removing a person** removes their sign-ins, Resume places and languages. Their stats and access-log lines stay, apps they linked keep their name until those apps are removed, their reports and their apps' problems stay until cleared, and backups keep everything until replaced.
- **It contacts** only your Plex, your alert addresses, Sonarr and Radarr, your own away-from-home address, and, if an Admin says yes, GitHub every 4 hours, sending only its version.

Plex's own privacy policy covers Plex.
