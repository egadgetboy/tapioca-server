# tapiOca's page

tapiOca's page is where Admins run the server. Open it at `http://<server-ip>:3310` at home, or at your own away-from-home address. It fits phones, tablets and computers.

[[screenshot: tapiOca's page on a computer, with the header, the tabs and the Stations tab]]

### The tabs

| Tab | What it's for | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stations** | Make, change and delete Stations; what's on; the guide. | Everyone |
| **Setup** | Tuner addresses, Media folders, picture and tuners, backups, new versions, the INFO station. | Admins |
| **Access** | People, devices, trusted networks and proxies, Viewing Levels, tuner access, away from home, the apps' limits and Media, API tokens. | Admins |
| **Stats** | How much each Station and title is watched; for Admins, the server's health and who's watching. | Everyone |
| **Logs** | What tapiOca is doing, the apps' problems, the access log, alert notifications. | Admins |
| **Broken files** | Files that can't play properly, people's reports, file checks, replacing files. Its count is what needs you. | Admins |

**The header** has small pills: Plex's state (**Plex connected**, or what's wrong; **Nothing to play yet** without Plex or a Media folder); tuners in use ("2 of 4 tuners in use · 3 connections", amber when every tuner is busy); and, for Admins, **Away from home** (**Up**, **Down**, **Checking**, **Can't check** or **One step left**), the number of alerts, and "tapiOca 1.45.0 is out" when a new version is. Your name and role, such as "Ada · Admin", opens **Your account**.

The tab row also has a **Buy me a coffee** link, shown to everyone. It opens the developer's page on buymeacoffee.com, and is never required.

**The footer** shows tapiOca's name and version, **Setup**, the copyright, **GNU AGPL v3**, **Source**, **Open-source notices** and **Privacy**. **Appearance** keeps the page **Light** or **Dark** in this browser; **Automatic** follows the device.

### Signing in

Once there's an Admin, everyone signs in with a **Name** and **Password**. At home, **Who's signing in?** lists the Admins who signed in on that browser with their password, each marked **PIN** (in Light sign-in) or **Password**. **Sign in with a password** is always there, for anyone. **Remove from this browser** takes you off the list. A User is never listed.

The page signs you out after an hour without activity. An Admin signed in only for watching (by a PIN away from home, or by a trusted network) sees why, with **Sign in with your password**.


### Your account

Choose your name at the top of the page to **Change your password** (your **Current password** and a **New password**, which signs you out everywhere else), to choose **How you sign in** (Admins: **Full** or **Light**, with **Your password, to change this**), to **Link an app**, or to **Sign out**.

### Linking an app with a code

On a TV, tapiOca's apps show a short code, such as `K7QM-4DPX`, so no one types a password with a remote.

1. On tapiOca's page, signed in with your password as the person who'll watch, choose your name, then **Link an app**. (The app may also show the page's `/link` address.)
2. Enter the **Code** and choose **Next**.
3. The page names the app, such as "tapiOca for Roku will be signed in as Ada." Link it only if it's yours and in front of you. Choose **Link**.

A code lasts 10 minutes. The app then appears under **Signed-in apps** on the **Access** tab.

### The page as an app

Add tapiOca's page to a home screen, or install it on a computer, to open it in its own window: in Safari on an iPhone or iPad, **Share → Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome on Android, **Add to Home screen**; on a computer, **Install** in Chrome's or Edge's address bar, or **File → Add to Dock** in Safari. Chrome and Edge install only from an `https://` address. Nothing is stored on the device: tapiOca must be reachable.
